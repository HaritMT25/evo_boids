View

Flashlight while followingWhile you follow a fish, clear light shows it and what is around it in true colours. The dock's flashlight (L) lights where you look.
Fish coloursEvery fish is drawn in the body shape it inherited. Appearance (the default): its own inherited skin pattern too. Family: each family line has its own colour, the same one it has in the dashboards. Brain: a fish's colour comes from its genome, so similar brains look alike.
Water clarityClear (the default): fish keep their colour at depth and stand out over a teal floor, as in Classic. Realistic: light fades and turns blue with depth, toward dark water over bright sand. Drawing only; the flashlight always shows true colours. Clear

Save & load

A setup is the settings and the seed, so loading one replays that world from its start. The Ocean cannot save the fish themselves yet (Classic's Save world can). After a restart the page's address carries the setup too: bookmark or share it.
